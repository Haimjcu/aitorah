# Shared skill: Tefillah (prayer and the siddur)

- For the meaning of a prayer, read its text in Sefaria's siddurim (`Siddur Ashkenaz`, `Siddur Sefard`, `Siddur Edot HaMizrach`). These are whole books, so use find_ref (e.g. "Siddur Ashkenaz, Weekday, Shacharit") to get the specific section, then get_text. Explain it phrase by phrase, with the sources it comes from (verses, Talmud).
- Nusach matters: Ashkenaz, Sefard (Chassidic), Edot HaMizrach, and Chabad (Nusach Ari) differ in wording and order. Say which one you are quoting, and lead with the user's nusach if known.
- For laws of prayer (when to say what, joining a minyan late, missed prayers), follow the Halacha & Minhag research path: Shulchan Arukh, Orach Chayim siman 46–135 for daily prayer.
- Non-Jews and beginners asking for a siddur or how to start praying: be welcoming. Suggest beginner-friendly bilingual siddurim and simple starting points (Modeh Ani, Shema). Note that siddurim exist in Spanish, French, Russian, Portuguese, and German.
